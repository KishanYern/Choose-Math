## What you'll find

**Take the career quiz**
Answer 8 honest questions about your interests and working style. Discover whether you're a Pure Mathematician, Applied Mathematician, Data Scientist, or Actuary at heart — with recommended courses and next steps tailored to your result.

**Explore 8 career paths**
From quantitative analyst to cryptographer, data scientist to AI researcher — each path includes real salary data sourced from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, a day-in-the-life schedule, required skills, top employers, and growth outlook.

**Browse top university programs**
A curated directory of influential mathematics departments (MIT, Princeton, UCLA, Carnegie Mellon, and more), filterable by pure math, applied math, and statistics focus.

**Read alumni stories**
7 profiles of mathematicians working at hedge funds, AI labs, insurance firms, and universities — what they studied, where they landed, and what they wish they'd known.

**Follow the roadmap**
A step-by-step timeline from high school through undergraduate and into your first math career, with honest advice at every stage.

**Track your progress**
A personal checklist of skills and milestones — from proof fundamentals and programming to internships and grad school prep — that saves automatically in your browser.

**Learn from the best resources**
25+ curated textbooks, online courses, competitions, tools, and communities for serious math students at every level.
